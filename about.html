About me.

I'm a Computer Engineering student at IOE Pulchowk Campus, specialising in AI and machine learning. I work as a Junior AI Engineer at Layman AI, where I started as an intern, mostly on computer vision: detection, tracking and pose, and making all of it hold up on real, messy footage.

At college my research sits on two sides of machine learning: using anomaly detection to catch attacks on networks, and studying how vision-language models can be hijacked by what they see. I also lead the AI side of the LOCUS Open Source Team for LOCUS 2027.

Off the keyboard, there's a decent chance I'm watching cricket (or shouting at it), out on my bike hunting for a road I haven't ridden yet, or in some corner of Nepal I decided to visit on short notice. There's photographic evidence. Music runs in the background through all of it.

The habit I can't switch off: when a random problem crosses my mind, I have to build some version of the solution. When I learn something worth keeping, I write it up on Medium.
