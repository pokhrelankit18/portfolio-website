Voice Bridge: Calls Without a Language Barrier

A video-calling app where each person speaks their own language and reads the other in theirs, as live captions.

Ankit Pokhrel
1 min read·2026·live appsource

The idea

Two people who don't share a language should still be able to just call each other. In Voice Bridge the other person's words show up as captions in your language while they talk, and what you type in chat arrives in theirs.

How it works

  • Peer-to-peer WebRTC video and audio (plus screen share), signalled over Socket.io.
  • Each caller streams their own mic to a FastAPI backend over a WebSocket, as 16 kHz PCM from an AudioWorklet.
  • The backend finds speech with voice-activity detection, transcribes it locally with faster-whisper, translates it into the other caller's language and pushes it back as a caption.
  • Translation is free and offline by default with Argos Translate; Google Cloud Translation is an opt-in for higher quality.
  • Translated text chat rides the same WebSocket, with typing helpers like phonetic transliteration for Hindi, Arabic and Japanese.

Honest limits

Two-party calls only, no auth, and state lives in memory, so a server restart drops the call. Captions arrive per phrase, after you pause, not word by word. Calls behind strict mobile NATs need TURN, so free relay servers are already wired in.
