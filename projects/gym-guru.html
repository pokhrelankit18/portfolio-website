Gym Guru: Five Minutes a Day, Better Form

A Flutter app that teaches one movement skill a day and coaches every rep through the camera.

Ankit Pokhrel
1 min read·2026

The idea

Most people quit training because sessions are long and nobody tells them their form is off. Gym Guru turns training into a short daily lesson: learn one skill like squat depth from a demo and three cues, practise a tracked set of 12 reps in front of the phone camera, then review a form score with errors flagged on the exact rep they happened.

Where it stands

It's a UI-complete prototype. Navigation, state and the whole lesson loop work. The camera feed and pose data are simulated: the skeleton is drawn from a depth curve, and scripted mistakes on rep 4 (knees cave in) and rep 9 (too shallow) exercise the coaching and results screens end to end.

Decisions

  • Each flow owns one ChangeNotifier, so there are no state-library dependencies while the data model settles.
  • Fonts ship with the app and runtime font fetching is off, so it renders correctly with no network.
  • Next: real pose estimation on device (ML Kit or MediaPipe), local persistence, then accounts.
