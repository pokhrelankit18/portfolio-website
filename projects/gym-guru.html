TOFU: An AI Trainer in Five Minutes a Day

An Android app that counts reps and checks form through the phone camera, coaches by voice, and turns short daily workouts into a game with a global leaderboard.

Ankit Pokhrel
2 min read·2026

The idea

Most people quit training because sessions run long and nobody checks their form. TOFU keeps workouts to about five minutes and gives feedback on every rep: pick a level, train in front of the phone, and earn stars, XP and a place on the leaderboard.

The workout flow

Every exercise gets its own short sequence: an animated demo with 2-3 cues, an I'm ready tap, a position check, a countdown, then tracking. Squats are done standing and bridges lying side-on, so the position check runs before each exercise, not once per workout. A help button replays the demo and pauses the set.

Tracking

  • Google ML Kit pose detection runs on the front camera. Frames are analysed in memory and never stored or uploaded.
  • Each exercise has its own pure-Dart analyzer: squat depth from how far the hips drop (calibrated to your standing pose), elbow and knee angles for push-ups and lunges, hip angle for bridges, graded 5-second segments for planks, arm and leg range for jumping jacks.
  • A setup gate waits until your body is in frame, clearly visible and still before counting. If it can't see you for 8 seconds it says what to fix.
  • I tuned the analyzers on real workout clips from a public Kaggle dataset (MediaPipe poses replayed through the Dart code). That moved thresholds, made reps count only from a proper start, and added a minimum body size after a ceiling fan was detected as a person.

Coaching without chatter

Speech is saved for moments that matter: each new exercise, a form mistake, "three more", and help when it can't see you. Clean reps and the countdown are short sounds I synthesised for the app. The same line repeats at most every 8 seconds and any two lines are at least 3.5 seconds apart. Voice and sounds can each be switched off.

Game and backend

  • Google sign-in with Firebase Auth. Setup asks only what the app uses: nickname, goal, experience, daily minutes and pain limits.
  • Stars from form score, XP with first-clear and boss bonuses, 6 ranks, 9 achievements, a daily-goal streak, and streak freezes earned with XP.
  • Knee or lower-back limits swap or ease exercises; the goal and your most common mistake pick a daily drill.
  • A weekly and all-time global leaderboard on Firestore. Without Cloud Functions the app writes its own totals, so security rules check every write: only you write your row, XP never drops, and one write adds at most one workout's XP.
  • Cache-first startup: Firestore's offline cache shows your progress instantly and syncs in the background.

Honest limits

Lunge and jumping-jack tracking is only tested on synthetic poses, since I found no matching real clips. Leaderboard scores are rule-checked, not validated on a server. Android only for now; iOS comes later.
