English to Nepali Translation, From Scratch

A 35M-parameter Transformer I wrote from the paper up and trained on a laptop, translating everyday English into fluent Nepali.

Ankit Pokhrel
1 min read·2026·live demosource

Why build it from scratch

I wanted to understand Transformers by building one with no translation library doing the hard parts: multi-head self and cross attention, sinusoidal positional encodings, the warmup learning-rate schedule, label smoothing. All of it is written by hand in PyTorch.

It started as Sanskrit

The first version translated Sanskrit to English, character by character. Ten epochs took about 18 hours on my M2 Air and produced phonetic mush. Around 80% of the attention compute was being spent on padding.

Switching to BPE tokens and length-bucketed batches made epochs about 12x faster and the English fluent, but the scores stayed in single digits. The model wasn't the problem. The corpus was 73k pairs of epic verse with loose, literary translations. In low-resource translation the data is the model, so I moved to a language pair with about 20 times more data and a standard benchmark.

What I built

  • An encoder-decoder Transformer: 4 + 4 layers, d_model 384, 8 heads, 35.0M parameters.
  • SentencePiece BPE with 16k pieces per language and byte fallback, so no word is ever unknown.
  • Data: NLLB's 19.6M mined English-Nepali pairs, cleaned and cut to the top 1M by LASER score.
  • Training: 6 epochs on an Apple M2 Air, about 2 hours each. Dev loss fell from 9.69 to about 4.55 with no overfitting.
  • Shipped as a Gradio app on Hugging Face Spaces.

Results

On FLORES-200 devtest (1,012 unseen sentences, greedy decoding): BLEU 13.78, ChrF 41.22. ChrF is the fairer judge here, because Nepali's rich inflection makes BLEU's exact-word matching read low. Its best translations are near-human:

EN  : In remote locations, without cell phone coverage, a satellite phone may be your only option.
REF : दुर्गम स्थानहरूमा, सेल फोनको कभरेज बिना, उपग्रह फोन तपाईंको एक मात्र विकल्प हुन सक्छ।
HYP : रिमोट स्थानहरूमा, सेल फोन कभरेज बिना, एक उपग्रह फोन तपाईंको मात्र विकल्प हुन सक्छ।
      BLEU 55.7 · ChrF 77.9

It still drops rare compound terms and borrows English loanwords like रिमोट where a native word exists. Beam search and more epochs would close part of that gap.
