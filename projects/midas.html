MIDAS: When a Road Sign Hijacks the Model

Reproducing an attack where one sign in the camera view flips a vision-language model's driving decision, then finding where the hijack lives inside the model.

Ankit Pokhrel
1 min read·2026

MIDAS is our undergraduate major project, and it's ongoing. We're reproducing CHAI, a published attack where a readable sign in a driving scene (say, one that reads PROCEED) makes a vision-language model change its decision, on InternVL2.5 with CHAI's DriveLM scenes. So far the attack reproduces, but more weakly than the paper reports, and a linear probe on the model's hidden states can already tell when it is following the sign instead of the scene. We've also compared model sizes from 1B to 8B, and next we want to try activation steering as a defense.
